The "Top X" columns show how many of each athlete's counting results (up to 5) fall in each rank band; bands are independent (each result counts in one column only). K1/C1: Top 3 = ranks 1–3, Top 6 = 4–6, Top 12 = 7–12, Top 20 = 13–20. Cross: Top 4 = 1–4, Top 8 = 5–8, Top 12 = 9–12, Top 16 = 13–16. Results outside these bands count in no column, so the Top X columns need not add up to 5.

The "Top N UN" column shows how many of the athlete's counting races (the races that make up their OQS points, up to 5) they finished in the top N unique nations (first N distinct nations by finish order in that race; where athletes tie on the boundary rank, all tied nations are credited). N = 6 for Kayak, 12 for Canoe, 3 for Kayak Cross.

Athletes are ranked by total OQS points, then by average finishing rank across their counting races. The average is shown rounded to the nearest whole number but ranked on its exact value, so two athletes can display the same average and still be separated. Results with no rank or no points recorded (DNS/DNF) are excluded.
